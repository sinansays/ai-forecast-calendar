Add a calendar

Choose subscription for ongoing corrections, or download a fixed copy.

Subscribe Keep a feed connected

  1. Copy the calendar’s subscription URL.
  2. In your calendar provider, find “Add calendar by URL,” “Subscribe to calendar,” or the equivalent.
  3. Paste the URL and confirm. Your provider decides how often it checks for updates.

Download Import a fixed copy

  1. Download the .ics file.
  2. Open or import it in your calendar application.
  3. Imported events do not update automatically; importing again may create duplicates.

Why no one-click subscribe button? Browsers and calendar providers handle subscription links differently. Copying the stable URL is the most reliable, provider-neutral approach.
